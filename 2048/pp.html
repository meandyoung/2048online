Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2024

1. Introduction

Welcome to 2048 online Game. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle any information when you visit our website and play our game.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide you with the best gaming experience:

  • Game scores and progress (stored locally in your browser)
  • Basic usage statistics
  • Device information (browser type, operating system)

3. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for:

  • Saving your game progress
  • Improving game performance
  • Analyzing general usage patterns
  • Maintaining and improving our service

4. Data Storage

Your game data is stored locally on your device using browser local storage. We do not store personal information on our servers.

5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your game progress. These cookies are necessary for the functionality of the game and do not track personal information.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics (to understand game usage)
  • Performance monitoring

These services may collect anonymous usage data according to their own privacy policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Our game is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
